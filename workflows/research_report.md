# Research Report Workflow

## Purpose

Produce a structured research report from user-provided sources or public information.

## Steps

1. Define the research question.
2. Collect sources and record URLs or file paths.
3. Compare claims across sources.
4. Separate facts, interpretation, and uncertainty.
5. Summarize findings.
6. Provide decision recommendations and next steps.

## Output

Use Markdown with sections for summary, evidence, comparison, risks, and recommendations.
