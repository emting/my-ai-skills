# Email Summary Workflow

## Purpose

Convert email content into a concise Markdown summary with decisions, tasks, risks, and follow-ups.

## Steps

1. Identify sender, recipients, date, and topic when available.
2. Extract key points.
3. Extract decisions.
4. Extract action items with owner and due date when available.
5. Flag risks, blockers, and missing information.
6. Produce a Markdown summary.

## Safety

Mask personal data when sharing summaries outside the authorized context.
