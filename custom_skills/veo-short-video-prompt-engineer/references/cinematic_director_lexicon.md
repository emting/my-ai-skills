# Google Veo 3.1 電影級光學與導演鏡頭語言詞典

## 1. 攝影機運鏡軌跡（Camera Trajectories & Motion）
- **Arc Shot (環繞運鏡)**: `Smooth 180-degree / 360-degree rotational arc shot around [Subject], maintaining focal lock.`
- **Dolly Zoom / Vertigo Effect (推拉變焦)**: `Dramatic dolly zoom, foreground subject stays static while background perspective warps rapidly.`
- **Tracking Follow / Lead (追隨／前導跟拍)**: `Low-angle tracking shot moving backward leading [Subject] through [Environment].`
- **Whip Pan (甩鏡轉場)**: `High-velocity horizontal whip pan blurring into the next dynamic focal plane.`
- **Crane / Jib Shot (懸臂俯衝／升降)**: `Sweeping crane shot descending vertically from high aerial perspective to an intimate eye-level shot.`
- **Steadicam Glide (穩定器滑行)**: `Fluid Steadicam shot weaving smoothly through a narrow corridor.`
- **Handheld Shaky-cam (紀錄片手持微晃)**: `Subtle handheld documentary camera motion with natural breathing jitter, adding tension and realism.`

## 2. 光學鏡頭與景深（Optics, Lenses & Focal Lengths）
- **35mm Anamorphic Lens (35毫米變形寬銀幕鏡頭)**: `Shot on 35mm anamorphic lens, 2.39:1 widescreen framing, horizontal blue streak flares, oval out-of-focus bokeh.`
- **50mm / 85mm Portrait Prime (大光圈定焦人像鏡)**: `85mm f/1.4 prime lens, razor-sharp focus on subject's eyes with ultra-shallow depth of field.`
- **Macro Lens (微距光學)**: `100mm macro lens revealing microscopic textures, water droplet surface tension, and skin pores in hyper-detail.`
- **Ultra-Wide 14mm / 16mm (超廣角透視)**: `14mm ultra-wide angle capturing expansive architectural geometry with dramatic perspective convergence.`
- **Rack Focus (焦點轉移)**: `Deliberate rack focus transition shifting crisp focal clarity from foreground glass reflections to the background character.`

## 3. 光影佈局與大師級照明（Lighting Design）
- **Rembrandt Lighting (林布蘭光)**: `Dramatic key lighting from high 45-degree angle creating a distinct inverted triangular highlight on the shadowed cheek.`
- **Chiaroscuro (明暗對照法)**: `High-contrast chiaroscuro lighting, deep cavernous shadows balanced against localized brilliant illumination.`
- **Volumetric God Rays (丁達爾體積霧光)**: `Atmospheric volumetric lighting beams cutting through airborne dust particles and atmospheric haze.`
- **Golden Hour / Blue Hour (晨昏魔幻時刻)**: `Warm golden hour low-angle sunlight casting elongated soft shadows / Cool desaturated twilight ambient light.`
- **Neon Cyberpunk / Dual Tone (雙色霓虹撞色)**: `Split lighting setup with vibrant electric cyan key light and contrasting magenta edge light.`

## 4. 色彩科學與底片質感（Color Science & Film Stocks）
- **Kodak Vision3 500T 5219**: `Warm highlight rendition, rich tungsten tones, organic fine-grained 35mm motion picture film texture.`
- **Bleach Bypass (銀鹽留銀工藝)**: `High-contrast silver retention look, desaturated pastel tones, accentuated edge sharpness, gritty industrial aesthetic.`
- **Technicolor 3-Strip (經典三色染印法)**: `Vibrant, deeply saturated primaries (red, green, blue) reminiscent of 1950s Hollywood technicolor cinema.`
- **Teal and Orange (現代好萊塢冷暖對比調色)**: `Cinematic complementary color grading, cool teal shadows opposing warm golden skin tones.`
